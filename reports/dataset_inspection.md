## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the schema, labels, data quality, and suitability of the three dataset sources present in the repository:
1. **Education Dialogue Dataset** (Hugging Face / Google Research)
2. **MathDial Dataset** (EPFL / GSM8K human-annotated math tutoring)
3. **Claude-Generated EduLens Datasets** (Synthetic student–LLM tutoring dialogues)

| Dataset Source | Records Available | Topics / Domain | Primary Labels Available | Usable for Supervised Learning of Topic Understanding? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Education Dialogue** | 47,234 (40k train, 7,234 eval) | School subjects (History, Bio, etc.) | **None** (No understanding or score labels present in files) | ❌ No (Unlabeled for supervised tasks) |
| **MathDial** | 2,861 (2,262 train, 599 test) | K-12 Math Word Problems | `self-correctness` (Yes / No / Revealed) | ⚠️ Supplementary only (Measures math problem-solving correction, not general topic understanding) |
| **EduLens Custom (`edulens_chats_dataset.json`)** | 430 complete sessions | Computer Science (11 subjects, 40+ topics) | `final_understanding` (`understood`, `partial`, `confused`), `quiz` | ✅ **Yes — Primary supervised dataset for EduLens topic understanding** |
| **EduLens Custom Sets 1–3 (`set1`, `set2`, `set3`)** | 1,290 sessions (420 + 430 + 440) | Multi-turn CS chat turns | `quiz` score only; no topic or understanding labels | ⚠️ Can be used for quiz regression or unlabeled pretraining, but lacks topic metadata for topic-wise intelligence |

## 2. Dataset A: Education Dialogue Dataset

- **Files:** `data/raw/education_dialogue/conversations_train1.json` through `train5.json`, `conversations_eval.json`
- **Total Records:** 47,234 conversations (40,000 train, 7,234 eval)
- **Provenance:** Synthetic dialogues generated by Gemini Ultra based on teacher/student pedagogical persona prompts (Multi-turn RLHF paper, arXiv:2405.14655).
- **Structure:**
  - `background_info`: Dict containing `topic`, `student_prefrences`, `teacher_prefrences`, `student_reactions`, `teacher_reactions`.
  - `conversation`: Array of turn objects `{"role": "Teacher" | "Student", "text": "..."}`.
- **Critical Finding — No Supervised Labels:**
  Although literature on this dataset mentions understanding probability, **no label, probability score, or post-assessment outcome exists in the raw JSON files**.
- **Role in Prototype:**
  Cannot be used for supervised training of a student outcome predictor. It remains preserved for potential future unsupervised language modeling or dialogue flow analysis.

## 3. Dataset B: MathDial Dataset

- **Files:** `data/raw/mathdial/train.jsonl` (2,262 records), `data/raw/mathdial/test.jsonl` (599 records)
- **Total Records:** 2,861 records
- **Provenance:** Human-annotated tutoring dialogues collected on Amazon Mechanical Turk for GSM8K math word problems (Macina et al., EPFL).
- **Structure:**
  - `qid`: Problem identifier
  - `question`: Word problem text
  - `ground_truth`: Mathematical solution
  - `student_incorrect_solution`: Initial erroneous student submission
  - `teacher_described_confusion`: Human teacher diagnosis
  - `conversation`: Multi-turn dialogue between tutor and student, delimited by `|EOM|`
  - `self-correctness`: Human annotator rating of whether the student corrected their mistake (`Yes`: 75.0%, `Yes, but I had to reveal the answer`: 13.4%, `No`: 11.2%)
  - `self-typical-confusion`: 1–5 Likert scale
- **Suitability Assessment:**
  - Contains genuine human annotations.
  - However, the domain is strictly elementary/middle-school math arithmetic problems, and the label (`self-correctness`) evaluates whether the student solved that specific arithmetic riddle, not conceptual mastery of a broader curriculum topic.
  - Highly imbalanced (88% positive resolution).
- **Role in Prototype:**
  Preserved in `data/raw/mathdial/`. Validates conversational analysis on human data, but not combined into the main EduLens CS understanding model to avoid invalid label mixing.

## 4. Dataset C: Claude-Generated EduLens Custom Datasets

### 4.1 Primary Dataset: `data/raw/edulens_chats_dataset.json`
- **Total Records:** 430 complete learning sessions
- **Provenance:** Generated with Claude with structured pedagogical prompts modeling realistic university student questions and learning struggles.
- **Top-Level Schema:**
  - `chat_id`: Unique identifier (e.g., `CHAT_0001`)
  - `student_id`: Fictional student identifier (e.g., `S01`)
  - `subject`: CS Subject area (11 distinct: DAA: 80, DSA: 80, ML: 63, OS: 50, DBMS: 40, C++: 27, CN: 27, Cloud: 18, Python: 18, Maths: 18, Java: 9)
  - `topic`: Granular topic (e.g., "Pointers and References", "Binary Search Trees", "Gradient Descent", "Virtual Memory")
  - `student_persona`: Learner profile (`beginner`: 107, `skeptical`: 78, `exam`: 69, `casual`: 68, `curious`: 65, `debug`: 43)
  - `turns`: Array of 3 to 7 message turns with `turn`, `student_message`, `question_type`, `cognitive_level`, `sentiment`, `confusion_flag`.
  - `final_understanding`: **Primary target label**
    - `understood`: 192 (44.7%)
    - `partial`: 156 (36.3%)
    - `confused`: 82 (19.1%)
  - `quiz`: Post-session quiz score (`num_questions`, `correct`, `score_percent`)
  - `overall_sentiment`: Synthetic sentiment (`positive`, `mixed`, `negative`)
- **Important Data Quality & Leakage Findings:**
  - `overall_sentiment` is 100% correlated with `final_understanding` by construction in the synthetic generation prompt. **It must never be used as a feature** to avoid target leakage.
  - Features must be extracted strictly from the raw `student_message` texts and natural conversation properties (message count, length, question markers, vocabulary), ensuring realistic inference on genuine student inputs.

### 4.2 Supplementary Sets: `set1 (1).json`, `set2 (1).json`, `set3 (1).json`
- **Record Counts:** 420, 430, 440 records respectively (1,290 records total).
- **Schema:** Contains `chat_id`, `student_id`, `timestamp`, `llm_client`, `messages`, and `quiz`.
- **Limitation:** They do NOT contain `subject`, `topic`, or `final_understanding` labels. They only contain synthetic `quiz` scores.
- **Preservation:** Intact in `data/raw/` for auditing and future regression or self-supervised experiments.

## 5. Architectural & Modeling Decision

1. **Prediction Target:** **Student Demonstrated Understanding Level (`final_understanding`)**
   - Classes: `understood` (Mastery), `partial` (Emerging / Incomplete), `confused` (Needs Intervention).
   - This directly fulfills the EduLens project goal of estimating student learning performance from conversation evidence.
2. **Eligible Training Data:**
   - `data/raw/edulens_chats_dataset.json` (430 sessions across 11 CS subjects and 40+ topics).
3. **Primary Model:**
   - **Multinomial Logistic Regression** with balanced class weighting, using Scikit-learn.
   - Paired with an interpretable NLP pipeline:
     - Word TF-IDF on student utterances (capturing conceptual terms vs. confusion cues).
     - Conversation structural features: number of turns, average word count per turn, question frequency, clarification phrase indicators.
   - Evaluated against a `DummyClassifier` (majority baseline) using stratified 5-fold cross-validation and a held-out test split.
4. **Dashboard Integration:**
   - Loads the single trained model artifact (`models/trained_model.joblib`).
   - Page 1: Evaluates any conversation (preset samples or custom pasted/uploaded text), extracts identical features, outputs class probabilities and predicted understanding level.
   - Page 2: Aggregates sessions by topic to display topic-wise student understanding distribution, average session length, and confusion signals.
   - Page 3: Transparently displays model architecture, features, and honest limitations.
