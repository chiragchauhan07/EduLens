Architecture & Preprocessing Contract

  • Primary Classifier: Multinomial Logistic Regression with balanced class weighting (C=1.0, max_iter=1000).
  • No Data Leakage: Preprocessing and TF-IDF transformers fitted strictly on training data inside a Scikit-learn Pipeline.
  • Exclusively Student Signals: AI/Teacher utterances are excluded to evaluate only student articulation.
  • Sub-Millisecond Inference: Runs in < 2ms without requiring expensive or nondeterministic LLM calls for classification.
